## Developing a sustained service practice

My aim is to move from expressions of interest to meaningful, sustained contributions, especially where technical skills, information systems or educational resources can support an organization's actual needs. Pursue opportunities for their community value, not merely for credentials.

For each completed assignment, record the organization, role, dates, verified hours if available, specific responsibilities, authorized deliverables, feedback and outcomes. Record applications separately from active assignments and completed work. Do not infer impact, service hours, selection, affiliation or continuous giving from registration or donor emails.
