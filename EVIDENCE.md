Status meanings:
- **Public artifact** — link exists; does not establish correctness, authorship of every component or measured impact.
- **Owner-reported** — requires primary evidence or review before presenting it as independently verified.
- **Demonstrated** — result supported by reproducible test or appropriate documentation; describe verification limits.
- **Independently verified** — named issuer/reviewer or reliable external evidence.
- **Planned** — not yet completed.
