## Decision gates
1. **Eligibility:** Obtain a written answer on the specific qualification; clarify credential-evaluation policy and whether bridging coursework is necessary. Domestic recognition and international equivalency are different.
2. **Academic fit:** Confirm programme-specific prerequisites and whether previous coursework satisfies them; plan formal credit only if required.
3. **Funding:** Confirm realistic assistantship/scholarship routes, first-semester availability, tuition coverage, stipends and uncovered costs; do not assume funding is guaranteed.
4. **Competitiveness:** Demonstrate MIS mastery, original technical work and research; prepare required tests and recommendations.
5. **Application:** Verify current intake policies, deadlines, documents and any immigration-related programme designation before submitting.

## State transition rules
Use **inquiry sent → response received → individual eligibility confirmed → formal application submitted → decision → funding confirmed** as distinct states. A university may decline eligibility or require further review; log the specific gate privately. Never advance a state based only on general admissions language or an automated receipt.
