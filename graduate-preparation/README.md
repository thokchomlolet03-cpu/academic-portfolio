# Graduate readiness
[Portfolio home](../README.md)

Target field: Management Information Systems / Information Systems. Long-term aim: turn professional experience and graduate study into useful research, resilient organizational systems and open learning resources.

Potential campus contributions, not positions held or guaranteed:
- Research assistance: reproducible experiments, data analysis, technical tooling.
- Teaching support: clear technical explanations, original practice material and peer support.
- Campus IT: enterprise support experience and demonstrated infrastructure skills.
- Innovation: prototypes for real organizational problems and responsible evaluation.

Build evidence before claiming readiness. Use the [MIS curriculum](https://github.com/thokchomlolet03-cpu/thokchomlolet03-cpu/tree/main/learning-hub/mis) as the academic workbench. For each university, privately tailor a short statement to its actual faculty research, coursework and needs. Any work or assistantship is subject to university eligibility and applicable authorization. Public portfolio supplements rather than replaces official application materials.
